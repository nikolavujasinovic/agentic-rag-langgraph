# Refund Policy

## Monthly plans
Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the
current billing period. Monthly plans are not eligible for partial or prorated refunds.

## Annual plans
Customers on annual plans may request a full refund within 30 days of the initial purchase or
renewal date. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, but customers can disable
auto-renewal at any time from the billing settings page.

## Enterprise contracts
Enterprise contracts are governed by the signed Master Services Agreement (MSA). The standard
30-day refund window does NOT apply to Enterprise; refunds are negotiated per contract by the
account executive together with Finance.

## Approval process
Support agents can approve refunds up to $1,000. Refunds between $1,000 and $5,000 require a
support team lead. Refunds above $5,000 require approval from the Finance department via the
#finance-approvals Slack channel. All refunds must be logged in the billing system with a reason code.
