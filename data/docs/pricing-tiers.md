# Pricing Tiers

## Starter
$29 per month. Up to 5 users, 3 projects, 10 GB storage and community support. No SSO.

## Pro
$99 per month (or $990 per year). Up to 50 users, unlimited projects, 500 GB storage, email
support with a 24-hour response target, and Google Workspace SSO.

## Enterprise
Custom pricing, billed annually. Unlimited users, 10 TB storage included, SAML SSO with any
identity provider, SCIM provisioning, a 99.95% uptime SLA, and a dedicated customer success
manager. Support response target is 1 hour for SEV1 issues.
